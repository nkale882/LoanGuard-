# Northwind Bank Consumer Lending Policy

This is a SYNTHETIC policy written for a tutorial project. The regulation
names (TILA, ECOA, FCRA, GLBA) are used illustratively. It is not legal advice
and not a real bank's policy.

## Loan Product Limits

| Product | Maximum Amount | Minimum Credit Score | Maximum APR | Approval Authority |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Personal Loan | 25000 | 640 | 24.99 | Loan Officer |
| Auto Loan | 60000 | 600 | 18.99 | Loan Officer |
| Home Equity Line | 250000 | 700 | 12.99 | Senior Underwriter |
| Student Refinance Loan | 80000 | 680 | 14.99 | Loan Officer |
| Small Business Line | 100000 | 690 | 16.99 | Senior Underwriter |

## Required Disclosures

| Situation | Required Disclosure | Regulation |
|---|---|---|
| Quoting an interest rate | APR including all fees | TILA |
| Declining an application | Adverse action notice with specific reasons | ECOA |
| Using a credit report in a decision | Credit score disclosure notice | FCRA |
| Offering a variable rate | Rate change schedule | TILA |
| Collecting application data | Privacy notice | GLBA |

## Prohibited Statements

| Statement Type | Rule |
|---|---|
| Guaranteeing approval | Prohibited before underwriting |
| Quoting a final rate before credit check | Prohibited |
| Citing marital status as a decision factor | Prohibited under ECOA |
| Promising a specific funding date | Prohibited without Operations confirmation |
| Waiving income verification | Prohibited |

## Processing Standards

- Adverse Action Notice Deadline Days: 30
- Application Acknowledgement Days: 5
- Income Verification Required Above Amount: 10000
